You produce a **low-fidelity text wireframe** of that page: structure, hierarchy, content and flow, with no visual styling. - Follow the direction's concept and layout archetype, and this page's purpose and sections, in order.

- Do not decide visual details (colors, fonts, shadows, radii).
- When the PM gave feedback on earlier sketches, apply it.
